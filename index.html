Dragon Kisser

BYU Senior Capstone · 2025–2026

Dragon Kisser is a boss-fight game centered around a chicken, Domingo, trying to romance a dragon, Luciana, through wacky means—whether dancing or one of the many minigames. I work on Team Luciana, implementing her AI behavior using Unreal State Trees and Behavior Trees, and more recently her Animation Blueprint to procedurally animate her neck, head, and eyes.

Nuked by Noon

CSANM 342: Real-Time Techniques / CSANM 494R: Special Problems · 2025–2026

Nuked by Noon is a stealth game where I handled all of the programming and collaborated with artists for visual assets. My main focus was designing an AI system that can detect, search for, and attack the player using Unreal’s State Trees and AI Perception, creating an enemy that reacts intelligently to the player’s movements and visibility.
