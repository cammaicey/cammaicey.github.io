About Me

Gameplay programmer focused on AI and systems.

I’m a computer science student at Brigham Young University specializing in animation and games. I enjoy building gameplay systems and game AI, and bringing ideas from prototype to a polished, playable experience. I’m especially interested in how enemy behavior, animation, and player interactions work together to make gameplay feel engaging.

How I work

I like digging into the details of how gameplay features work—from structuring AI behavior and connecting systems to iterating on timing and player feedback. I value clear communication with artists and other programmers, documenting technical workflows, and testing features in-game to make sure they feel right as well as function correctly.
