A little about me

Making games feel alive.

I’m a gameplay programmer studying Computer Science with emphasis in Animation and Games at Brigham Young University. I love making an idea into a reality. Creating an experience that feels engaging and fun is always my goal.

What I Enjoy Building

AI & behavior

Reactive Character Logic

Building character logic that handles changing gameplay situations, manages states, and drives behavior dynamically.

Gameplay Systems

Mechanics That Feel Right

Designing how gameplay systems fit together, then iterating on them through playtesting until they feel intuitive, responsive, and fun.

Animation & polish

Bringing Worlds To Life

Connecting gameplay logic to visual and auditory feeback is one of my favorite ways to make characters and environments feel alive.

My Approach

I’m happiest when I can take a feature from an early idea to something working in-game. That usually means experimenting, debugging, playtesting, adjusting the feel, and collaborating with the people building the art and animation around it.

I’m also a teaching assistant for Unreal Engine coursework, which gives me the chance to help other students troubleshoot their projects and explain technical concepts. It’s been a good reminder that solving a problem and helping someone understand how to solve it are both valuable parts of programming.

What I’m Working Toward

I want to keep growing as a gameplay programmer and contribute to games with memorable combat, interesting AI, and mechanics that encourage players to engage and experiment. I’m especially excited to keep learning from other developers and working on teams where ideas can evolve through iteration.

Thanks for taking a look at my work!
