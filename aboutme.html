A little about me

Keeping the spirit of gaming alive.

I’m a gameplay programmer studying Computer Science with an emphasis in Animation and Games at Brigham Young University. I grew up watching my older siblings play video games from a very young age and quickly fell in love. In 2008, I got my first gaming system, a metallic rose DS Lite. I remember that moment so vividly. How excited I was, how I much fun I had playing, how I very quickly broke part of the carrying case my parents bought for it. From lurking over my siblings shoulders as a kindergartener to developing and releasing a succesful game as a college student, my life has been forever impacted by the game community.
